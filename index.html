Source links & methodology

Model does not infer gasoline pump spikes on Fridays from weekly data. It also does not turn notices of planned bridge closures into measured hours of congestion. Previous-year household expenses cannot be reconstructed accurately from a current county living-wage estimate alone.

Job collection anchored to 60432 with a 35-mile straight-line ZIP-radius screen; real road mileage is separately supplied. The local gasoline record is Chicago-area regional data, not 60432 measured station prices. Historical legal rates exclude worker-specific exemptions.
